Methodology: Data are generated for portfolio demonstration; not actual sales. Measures are calculated from 239 order-item rows joined to 120 orders, 40 customers and 10 products. Only Completed orders contribute to financial KPIs. Selecting a category shows its portion of order revenue, so AOV then means filtered-category revenue divided by orders containing that category. The dashboard is an interactive HTML preview; the downloadable project includes Power BI-ready CSV, DAX measures, theme and build guide, not a native PBIX.
