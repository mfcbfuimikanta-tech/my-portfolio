02 / PROJECT APPROACH

From relational data to insight

01 — Combine

Mapped four linked tables: customers, products, orders and order items. Built one Power BI-ready sales fact table with original transaction-level prices.

02 — Measure

Created DAX measures for completed revenue, distinct completed orders, average order value, sold quantity, purchasing customers and completion rate.

03 — Visualize

Designed KPI cards, trend line, product rankings, city comparisons, category mix, segment breakdown and a detailed product table.

04 — Validate

Reconciled calculated metrics against the earlier SQL project: 120 orders, 91 completed and $6,421 in completed revenue.

04 / BUSINESS INSIGHTS

What the numbers reveal

Sunscreen leads product revenue at $1,159, followed by Vitamin C Serum at $1,144. Mukalla leads city revenue with $2,434 (about 37.9% of the total), and December is the highest revenue month at $1,110. In a real business, I would examine whether these patterns persist over a longer period and reconcile returns, discounts and payment status before making operational recommendations.
